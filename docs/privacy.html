TripMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

TripMate turns a city and dates into a day-by-day trip plan. This page explains what information the app uses, why, who else sees it, and how you can delete it. In short: we only use what's needed to make and save your plans, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Your email address and passwordOnly if you create an account To log you in. Your password is stored securely (hashed) by our login provider, Supabase; we never see it.
Trip requests: country, city, dates, number of travellers, travel style, how you get around, and (optional) your hotel or areaEach time you tap “Plan my trip” To make your plan. We don't keep these unless you save the trip.
Saved tripsWhen you tap “Save this trip” So you can open them again under “My trips”. They're linked to your account.
A record of each AI plan you make (your account ID and the time)Each AI plan, when logged in To apply the daily limit on AI plans.
Technical information such as your IP addressEvery time the app contacts a server Needed to send the app's data over the internet; also kept briefly in server logs to keep the service working and secure.

TripMate does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Who else sees it

We use these services to run TripMate. Each only gets what it needs for its job:

  • Supabase – login and our database (your email, saved trips and AI plan records).
  • Render – hosts the TripMate server that your app talks to. Its logs can briefly include the city or hotel name you searched for.
  • Resend – sends TripMate’s account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password). It receives your email address.
  • Expo – hosts the TripMate website.
  • GitHub – hosts this page.
  • Anthropic (Claude) – writes AI trip plans. It receives your trip request (city, dates, travellers, style, transport and hotel or area), but not your email or account.
  • Google Places – checks that places are real and open, and finds your hotel. It receives place names, the city and your hotel or area. Place photos in the app are loaded directly from Google, so Google can see your IP address.
  • MET Norway and Open-Meteo – weather forecasts, finding a city on the map, and suggesting city names as you type. They receive the city (as its name, the letters typed so far, or its approximate map position), sent by our server, never your own location.
  • open.er-api.com – exchange rates. It receives no information about you.
  • YouTube – only if you tap “Watch on YouTube”, which opens a YouTube search for that place.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Saved trips: until you delete them, or delete your account.
  • Your account and AI plan records: until you delete your account.
  • Trip requests that you don't save: not kept, apart from brief server logs.
  • Place information from Google (not linked to you) is kept for up to 30 days so we don't look up the same place again.

Deleting your information

In the app, log in and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, all your saved trips and your AI plan records. You can also delete single trips under “My trips”. Your login is kept on your phone until you log out or delete your account.

Children

TripMate is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
